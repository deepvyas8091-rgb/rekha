# Rekha: competitor gaps, fix plan and positioning

Sample: 142 public reviews of 6 competing apps (Astrotalk 50, AstroSage 43, Drik Panchang 23, three palm-reading apps 26), from App Store India and US (86), Trustpilot (42), Google Play India (9) and one forum thread (5). Collected 16 Oct 2026. Every quote below is verbatim and linked. Where a review had a title, the title and the visible text are joined with a full stop. Star ratings were visible only on Trustpilot (43 reviews); App Store and Play pages hid them. Files: `reviews.csv` (all rows), `feedback.md` (ranked themes).

Limits of the sample: the store pages show only the first 3 to 10 reviews each, so this is what the stores choose to show, not a random sample. Reddit and Apple's review feed could not be reached. The palm apps are US apps; no India or Southeast Asia palm-app reviews were found.

## Where Rekha already stands

Rekha (rekha.io) today: free Choghadiya, Rahu Kaal, Hora, Panchang and festival calendar for any city, a Vedic kundli with dasha and Sade Sati, 36-gun matching, a daily Moon-sign horoscope, palm reading (lines read by AI from a photo, then deleted) and face reading (measured on the phone). It works in 15 languages, installs from the browser, has no ads, no human astrologers, no wallet and no shop. Premium is a flat monthly plan per country (Solo, Couple, Family). The AI is told never to use fear, never to push remedies or gemstones, and to say what it could not see.

That means several of the loudest competitor complaints are things Rekha already avoids by design. The opportunity is to make that visible and provable, and to fix the places where Rekha could fall into the same traps.

## 1. What they hate

1. **Pressure to pay: wallets, recharges, per-minute fees, paywalls before any result.** 28 reviews, 4 source types, 5 of 6 apps.
   - "they are just looting people by influencing them to add money to their wallets" ([AstroSage, App Store IN](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/astrosage-kundli-ai-astrology/id824705526?see-all=reviews))
   - "You can’t read unless you share or buy. Just be upfront and say so. Why am I going share or buy something if I don’t see what I’m getting?" ([Palm Reader: Palmistry Life, App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/palm-reader-palmistry-life/id1468487172?see-all=reviews))
2. **Distrust: "scam", "fraud", "fake", playing on fear.** 15 reviews, 3 source types, Astrotalk and AstroSage.
   - "They are fake. They harp of people's fear and anxiety." ([AstroSage, Trustpilot, 1★](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6877f94d0a31383822228dc5))
   - "show how the app AstroTalk take advantage of their sentiment and extort money from them." ([TechEnclave forum](https://techenclave.com/t/astrotalk-a-fraud/268655))
3. **Wrong or generic predictions.** 14 reviews, 2 source types, 3 apps.
   - "I was really hoping for genuine insight, but this felt completely generic and scripted." ([Astrotalk, Trustpilot, 1★](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6aa18f17c16064a9f5cb2140))
   - "I talked to two readers and none of the numerous predictions came true." ([AstroSage, Trustpilot, 1★](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/699c29c4d3d45410b91d4f46))
4. **AI passed off as people.** 7 reviews, 2 source types, Astrotalk and AstroSage. Recent: all but one are from 2026.
   - "It’s only AI at this point with only a couple of real people" ([Astrotalk, Trustpilot, 1★](https://www.trustpilot.com/reviews/6a94f753901df7040ce91fd9))
   - "AI has absolutely ruined this app. This used to be my go to app to look at my vedic chart but with the new AI that has been implemented," ([AstroSage, App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/astrosage-kundli-ai-astrology/id824705526?see-all=reviews))
5. **Wrong sign or calculation.** 6 reviews, 2 source types, 3 apps. Several are the Western versus Indian (sidereal) sign difference, which the apps never explain.
   - "Wrong Kundli Reading. My sun sign as per my birth date is Cancer, however as per my kundli, it is Sagittarius." ([Astrotalk, App Store IN](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/astrotalk-talk-to-astrologer/id1208433822?see-all=reviews))
   - "It showed Aquarius even though I entered Pisces." ([Palm Reader: Palmistry Life, App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/palm-reader-palmistry-life/id1468487172?see-all=reviews))
6. **Ads and pop-ups (Drik Panchang, AstroSage).** 5 reviews, 2 source types.
   - "Please add a low-cost yearly ad-free plan. The ads are becoming irritating, and sometimes they appear" ([Drik Panchang, Google Play IN](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.drikp.core&hl=en_IN))
7. **Rude or biased astrologers, no moderation.** 5 reviews, 2 source types, AstroSage only. Not relevant to Rekha unless it adds human astrologers.
8. **Customer service and product orders (bracelets, "Vastu combo").** 10 reviews, THIN: Trustpilot only. Rekha sells no products.
9. **Badly timed palm scans.** 4 reviews, THIN: App Store only, palm apps only.
   - "It scans it wild and too quickly." ([Palm Reader: Palmistry Life, App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/palm-reader-palmistry-life/id1468487172?see-all=reviews))

## 3. What is unsolved (whole groups the apps ignore)

1. **People who want the tools, not a marketplace.** The free-tool users praise depth ("I hvnt find the yoga and karana anywhere", [Drik Panchang, App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hindu-calendar-drik-panchang/id1321271821?see-all=reviews)) while the consult apps push wallets and stones. Nobody combines Drik-level timings with readings and no upsell. Evidence: paywall theme 28 plus scam theme 15.
2. **People who want honest AI.** The anger is about hidden AI and generic text, not AI itself. No competitor shows where a reading comes from. Evidence: AI theme 7 plus generic/wrong theme 14.
3. **Indians abroad and the diaspora.** "Living in US, I had always felt the disconnect for not been able to get astrologer's advice" ([Astrotalk, App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/astrotalk-talk-to-astrologer/id1208433822?see-all=reviews)). One review: THIN, but it matches Rekha's world city search, local currency and languages.

## The angle

Option A: no upsell (recommended).

For people who are tired of astrology apps that push wallets, per-minute astrologers and gemstones, Rekha gives the full daily timings and your readings at one flat price, and shows you what you get before you pay.
Evidence: pay pressure, 28 reviews across 4 source types; trust, 15 across 3.

Option B: honest AI.

For people who distrust astrology apps that pass AI off as people, Rekha says plainly that its readings are AI-written from calculated facts and shows the working behind every line.
Evidence: AI theme, 7 across 2; generic or wrong predictions, 14 across 2.

Option C: the clean Panchang.

For Choghadiya and Panchang users fed up with ads and broken widgets, Rekha gives ad-free timings for their exact city, in their language, with alerts.
Evidence: ads, 5 across 2; widgets, 4 across 1 (thin).

Recommendation: lead with A, and use B as the proof. A is backed by the largest themes and is already how Rekha works; B is the hardest to copy and turns Rekha's main risk (being "just AI") into the reason to trust it. Keep C for Panchang search pages, where the people searching are the Drik Panchang audience.

Do not use competitor names in Rekha's name, ads or store listing. A comparison page is a question for a lawyer.
