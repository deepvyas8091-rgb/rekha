# Backlink outreach kit

Goal: move the daily pages ("rahu kaal today", "choghadiya today <city>") from positions 10 to 14 into the top 3. For a young domain, that takes links from relevant, trusted sites. The free widget at https://rekha.io/widgets is the offer: a useful daily box for their visitors, with a plain text link back to the city page under it.

## Who to contact (in order of value)

1. **Hindu temples outside India.** Websites in Toronto, Brampton, Mississauga, New Jersey (Edison), Houston, Dallas, Chicago, Bay Area, London, Leicester, Birmingham, Sydney, Melbourne, Perth, Auckland, Dubai, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Nairobi and Durban. Many publish a monthly calendar by hand. Offer the widget for their home page or calendar page.
2. **Indian associations and student groups.** City associations (Gujarati Samaj, Marathi Mandal, Bengali Association, Tamil Sangam, Telugu Association) and Indian student associations at universities. Their event pages already link to festival resources.
3. **Community news and listings sites for the Indian diaspora**, plus city WhatsApp and Facebook groups that keep a "useful links" page.
4. **Astrology and Vastu practitioners' blogs** that do not compete on timings.
5. **Wedding and puja service providers** (pandit booking, puja samagri shops) that would rather link to a timings tool than build one.

Skip link sellers, private blog networks and paid guest posts. They risk a Google penalty and do not help a trust-sensitive niche.

## How to find them

- Google: `hindu temple <city> website`, `<city> gujarati samaj`, `<city> hindu society calendar`, `site:.org "panchang" <city>`.
- Look for pages that already show Rahu Kaal or festival dates typed in by hand. Those owners get the most from an automatic widget.
- Check Rekha's own Search Console: the cities with the most impressions (Toronto, Perth, Kuwait City, Atlanta, Philadelphia) come first.

## Tracking

Keep a sheet with: organisation, city, contact, date sent, reply, page where the widget went live, and whether the text link under it is present. Ten live placements on temple or association sites in the main diaspora cities is a good first target.
